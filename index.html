BEFORE YOU GO

A few good
questions.

Can I request a custom trip? +

Yes. Use the inquiry form to share your destination ideas, dates, group size and interests. Custom requests are inquiries only until details and availability are confirmed.

Are trips available to book online now? +

This is the first website prototype. Real trip dates, pricing, availability, payments and confirmations still need to be connected to the business's booking process before accepting reservations.

Do I need hiking experience? +

That depends on the route and activity. When a trip is listed, its distance, elevation, terrain, difficulty and preparation requirements should be clearly explained.

Are you operating in every U.S. destination? +

The destination pages are a starting point, not a claim of current operations. Each offered trip must be confirmed with qualified guides, required permits, insurance and local arrangements.
